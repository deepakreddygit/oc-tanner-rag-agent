# Acme Corp Customer Policies

_Last updated: January 2026_

This document describes Acme Corp's customer-facing policies for orders placed through
acmecorp.com or any authorized Acme Corp retail partner. It is the single source of truth
for customer support agents and automated support tooling. If a situation is not covered
here, escalate to a human supervisor rather than guessing.

## 1. Returns & Refunds

Customers may request a refund within **30 days of the delivery date** shown on the
shipment tracking record. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7
business days of Acme Corp receiving the returned item at our processing center.

A **10% restocking fee** applies to **opened electronics priced over $500** (for example,
laptops, monitors, and home theater systems). The restocking fee does **not** apply to:

- Unopened electronics in original, unopened packaging, regardless of price.
- Any item outside the Electronics category (Home Goods, Apparel, Office Supplies, etc.),
  opened or unopened.
- Items that arrived defective or damaged in transit (see Section 4, Damaged & Defective
  Items).

To start a return, customers submit a return request through their Acme Corp account or
by contacting Customer Support with their order number. Acme Corp provides a prepaid
return shipping label for defective items; for other returns, the customer is responsible
for return shipping costs unless they hold Acme Plus membership (see Section 6).

Refunds are not available after 30 days from delivery under any circumstance, including
store credit. Exchanges (swapping an item for a different size or color of the same
product) may be requested up to 45 days from delivery and are not subject to the
restocking fee.

## 2. Shipping

Acme Corp ships to all 50 U.S. states and to Canada. Standard shipping within the
continental United States takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50; orders
under $50 incur a flat $5.99 shipping fee. Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is
available at checkout for an additional fee calculated by weight and destination.

Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, and Canada may take 5-10 business days and are subject to
additional carrier surcharges shown at checkout. Acme Corp does not currently ship
outside the United States and Canada.

Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number by email. If a package
shows as delivered but the customer has not received it, Customer Support will open a
carrier investigation; a replacement or refund is issued if the investigation confirms
the package was lost.

## 3. Order Cancellations & Changes

Orders can be cancelled or modified (shipping address, item quantity) free of charge
within 1 hour of purchase, as long as the order has not yet entered "Processing" status
in the fulfillment system. Once an order enters Processing, it cannot be cancelled or
redirected; the customer must wait for delivery and initiate a standard return instead.

## 4. Damaged & Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or is defective on arrival, the customer must report it to
Customer Support within 7 days of delivery, ideally with photos of the damage. Acme Corp
covers return shipping in full for damaged or defective items and will issue a full
refund or a free replacement, at the customer's choice, with no restocking fee.

## 5. Warranty

All electronics sold by Acme Corp carry a manufacturer's warranty of at least 1 year from
the date of purchase, covering defects in materials and workmanship. Acme Corp also
offers an optional Acme Care extended warranty (2 or 3 year terms) purchasable at
checkout, which additionally covers one incident of accidental damage. Warranty claims
are handled by contacting Customer Support with proof of purchase; approved claims are
resolved by repair, replacement, or refund at Acme Corp's discretion.

Warranty coverage is void if damage results from unauthorized modification, misuse, or
use of the product outside its intended purpose.

## 6. Acme Plus Membership

Acme Plus is a paid membership program that includes free standard shipping on all
orders regardless of order size, free return shipping labels on all returns, and early
access to seasonal sales. Membership is billed annually and can be cancelled at any time
from the customer's account settings; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing period, and no partial-period refunds are issued for membership fees.

## 7. Price Adjustments

If an item a customer purchased drops in price on acmecorp.com within 7 days of their
purchase, the customer may request a one-time price adjustment (refund of the
difference) by contacting Customer Support. Price adjustments are not available during
Acme Corp's two major sale events (Spring Clearance and Winter Blowout), and are limited
to one adjustment per order.

## 8. Customer Support Contact

Customer Support is available Monday-Friday, 8am-8pm Eastern Time, and Saturday,
9am-5pm Eastern Time, via chat, email (support@acmecorp.example), and phone. Response
time for email inquiries is typically under 24 hours on business days.
